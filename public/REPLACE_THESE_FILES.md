# Drop your logo files here

## logo.png
- Used in the sidebar on every page
- Recommended size: 160 x 40px (wide/horizontal layout)
- Format: PNG with transparent background, or SVG renamed to logo.svg (update Sidebar.tsx import)

## logo-intro.mp4
- Plays ONCE when a user first lands on the dashboard after login
- After it finishes (or after 6 seconds), it fades out automatically
- Recommended: 3–6 seconds, 1080x1080 or 1920x1080, MP4 format
- Also provide a WebM version named logo-intro.webm for better browser support (optional but recommended)

## favicon.ico
- Browser tab icon — must be .ico format, 32x32 or 64x64
- Replace the one in app/favicon.ico with your own
